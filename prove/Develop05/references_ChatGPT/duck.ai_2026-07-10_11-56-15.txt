In c#, do Private methods get inherited by child classes?

GPT-5.4 nano:
No. In C#, `private` methods are **not inherited** by child classes, so a derived class can’t call them directly and they don’t show up as inherited members.

What *is* inherited depends on access:
- `protected` → inherited and callable in derived classes
- `public`/`internal` (or `protected internal`) → inherited as well (with the usual accessibility rules)
- `private` → not inherited; you’d need to expose it (e.g., via `protected`/`public`) or use other patterns
